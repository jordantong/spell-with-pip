Backup

Her lists, gems, stickers and room are saved inside this home-screen app. Updates never erase them — new versions load in place. They are lost only if the app icon is deleted and added again, or the iPad is reset. So before ever deleting the icon, save a backup here (to Files or iCloud Drive) and restore it afterwards.

Gems and the Shop

She earns 1 gem per word in practice or a practice test, 3 gems per word in a Power Round, plus bonus gems for streaks and the daily treasure. Gems are spent in Pip's Shop on furniture for Pip's house (20–150), wall stickers (10–35), things for Pip to wear (25–120), wallpaper and floors (15–30), and new rooms: the kitchen (250), garden (450) and playroom (700).

Privacy

  • Everything Pip knows — her name, word lists, gems and history — is stored on this device only. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no third-party services. The fonts and sounds are built into the app, so it makes no outside requests once it's open.
  • The voice comes from this device's own speech settings. Nothing she types or hears is sent anywhere.
  • Added to the Home Screen, Pip works offline. The web address it came from is public, and that host sees a normal web request (IP address and time) when the app is first downloaded or later updated.
  • Because her data lives only here, clearing the browser's website data or deleting the icon also deletes her progress. Use “Save a backup” in the installed app to keep a copy.
